## 📖 Story & Historical Overview

**120 Brave Indian Soldiers. 1 Desert Night. 45 Enemy Tanks.**

Set during the decisive night of **04–05 December 1971** in the Thar Desert, *THAR FRONTIER: A Soldier's Promise* puts players in the boots of **Havaldar Devrath Singh** of the **23 Punjab Regiment**. 

When emergency sirens blare across Jaisalmer Railway Station, Devrath's leave is revoked. Holding a treasured letter from his mother in Shekhawati, he returns to the forward border outpost of Longewala, bound by a holy promise to survive the freezing sandstorms and return home to his infant daughter Kavya.

Under the commanding leadership of **Major Kuldip Singh Chandpuri (MVC)** and alongside **Subedar Bhairon Singh (SM)** of the Border Security Force, fight through 18 historical missions until dawn, when the **Hawker Hunters** of the Indian Air Force roar across the desert horizon.

---

## 🎮 Key Features

- **Cinematic 2.5D Thar Parallax:** Multi-layer handcrafted desert dunes, Khejri trees, sandstone rocks, barbed-wire border pillars, and authentic 1971 Jaisalmer railway platforms.
- **Visceral Tactical Combat:** Master Devrath's 7.62mm 1A1 SLR battle rifle, complete with silica sand-jam clearing QTE mechanics, precision aim, and close-quarters bayonet/melee combat.
- **106mm M40 RCL Anti-Tank Warfare:** Commandeer the 106mm Recoilless Rifle mounted on an Indian Army 4x4 Jeep to ambush enemy armor columns.
- **18 Historical Missions Across 4 Acts:**
  - **Act I: The Revoked Horizon (M1–M4):** The station recall, desert convoy, and Tanot Mata blessings.
  - **Act II: The Iron Mirage (M5–M10):** Scout vigilance, anti-tank mine traps, and BP 638 outpost clash.
  - **Act III: The Night of Longewala (M11–M14):** 106mm RCL ambush, trench defense, silica sand jam, and shell-shock trauma recovery.
  - **Act IV: The Dawn of Hunters (M15–M18):** IAF Hawker Hunter close air support, tank graveyard counter-assault, and the promise fulfilled.
- **Procedural Audio & Folk Soundtrack:** Real 1971 military sound effects, Web Audio air-raid sirens, tank diesel rumbles, and Rajasthani folk melodies.

## 💻 Standalone Windows PC Game (.EXE)

The game is packaged as a direct, standalone Windows installer executable (**no zip extraction needed**):

1. Download **`TharFrontier1971_Setup.exe`**.
2. Double-click the installer and click **"Install & Play"**.
3. It installs to your PC, automatically creates a **Desktop Shortcut** with the official Havaldar Devrath badge icon, and launches the game in 60 FPS full-screen.

## ☁️ Deploying to Vercel

This repository includes a pre-configured `vercel.json`:

1. Go to [Vercel Dashboard](https://vercel.com/new).
2. Import this GitHub repository: `mrrathoretech602/Thar-Fronteir`.
3. Keep default settings and click **Deploy**.
4. Vercel will automatically serve:
   - **`/`** &rarr; Official Showcase & Download Portal (`download.html`)
   - **`/game`** &rarr; Playable Web Edition (`index.html`)
   - **`/download`** &rarr; Direct Setup.exe download link
